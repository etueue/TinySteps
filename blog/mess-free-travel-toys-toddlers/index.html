BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Containment and Footprint

When space is limited to a 10x15 inch tray table, containment is everything. Prioritize toys that come with their own storage tin or case. Magnetic components or tethered pieces are superior because they prevent the 'fallen toy' drama in a dark cabin.

The Decibel Count

A quiet environment is a shared luxury on a flight. Opt for toys with a focus on tactile feel—snaps, clicks, and textures—rather than those with high-pitched electronic sounds. If choosing an electronic toy, ensure it has a reliable volume control or a headphone jack.

Low-Maintenance Materials

Travel is messy. Look for non-porous materials like ABS plastic or coated wax that can be wiped down easily. Avoid toys with too many loose parts or those that require liquids or glues, as these are difficult to manage in transit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What toys are strictly forbidden by TSA or airlines?

Generally, avoid anything that looks like a weapon, contains large amounts of liquid (over 3.4oz), or has strong magnets that could interfere with electronics (though small magnetic toys like Magna-Tiles are perfectly fine).

How do I clean sticky residue off travel toys?

For plastic or magnetic toys, a simple alcohol-free baby wipe is usually enough. For wax-based toys like Wikki Stix, use a dry cloth; water can actually make them more slippery.

Are LCD tablets safe for a toddler's eyes?

Yes, most LCD writing tablets use reflected light rather than a back-lit blue light screen, meaning they have a look similar to paper and do not cause the same eye strain as an iPad or phone.
