BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Material Integrity

Look for solid woods like beech, maple, or birch. Avoid 'wood-look' plastics or thin MDF that can peel. Natural wood is not only more durable but also possesses natural antimicrobial properties.

Storage Footprint

In small homes, the best toys are those that nest or include their own storage (like a canvas bag or a hollow core). Measure your shelving height to ensure 'vertical' toys will fit when not in use.

Cleaning & Maintenance

Skip the harsh chemicals. A damp cloth with a drop of mild soap is usually enough for finished wood. Avoid soaking wooden toys, as this can cause the grain to swell and the wood to crack.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these toys safe for teething toddlers?

Yes, our selected items use non-toxic, water-based finishes and natural woods. However, always check for small parts (like the counting animals) that may be better suited for children 3+ who have moved past the 'mouth-everything' phase.

Why choose wood over plastic for STEM play?

Wood provides a consistent weight and tactile resistance that helps children understand gravity and balance more intuitively than lightweight, hollow plastic.

How do I store 142 small sticks without it looking like clutter?

The PJojoqa set includes a storage bag, but for a truly curated look, we recommend a small wicker basket or a ceramic bowl that lives on a low, accessible shelf.
