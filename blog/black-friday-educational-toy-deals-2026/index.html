BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Think Horizontal

When you have limited counter or shelf space, look for toys that stack flat or fit into standard-sized storage bins.

Material Integrity

Prioritize wood or high-density cardboard over plastic. They last longer, feel better, and hold their value.

Maintenance Ease

Always check if the items are easy to wipe down. A simple damp cloth is all you should ever need.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I deal with toy clutter?

Adopt the 'rotate' method. Keep 75% of the toys out of sight in a closet and swap them every two weeks to keep play fresh and your counters clear.

Are wooden toys really safer?

Generally, yes. They are more durable, don't harbor as much bacteria as cheap plastic, and avoid off-gassing issues. Just look for non-toxic paint certifications.

What's the best way to clean these?

For wood, use a light mist of water and a microfiber cloth. Avoid soaking them, as it can warp the natural grain over time.
