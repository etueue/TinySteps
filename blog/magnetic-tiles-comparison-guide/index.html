BUYER'S GUIDE

What to Consider Before You Choose

Magnet Strength & Safety

Ensure the tiles feature ultrasonic welding rather than just glue. This ensures magnets stay encapsulated, which is vital for safety and ensuring your structures don't collapse mid-build.

Storage as Part of the Design

Consider how you'll store them before buying. Sets that include a storage tin or bin help maintain that clutter-free aesthetic your home deserves.

Piece Geometry Matters

A higher number of right triangles and squares is generally more useful for architectural builds than having too many specialty shapes, which can limit your child's creative freedom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all magnetic tile brands compatible?

Most brands use the same standard dimensions, but check the edge design. Standard tiles from major brands like Magna-Tiles are generally compatible with generic sets, but strength can vary slightly.

How do I clean magnetic tiles?

Because they are non-porous ABS plastic, you can wipe them down with a damp cloth or a mild, non-toxic surface cleaner. Avoid submerging them, as water can potentially seep into the edges.

Is the investment in the original brand worth it?

If you want consistent magnet strength and high-quality refraction, yes. If you are building a vast collection for a playroom floor, mixing generic sets for quantity and original sets for the 'special' pieces is a common strategy.
